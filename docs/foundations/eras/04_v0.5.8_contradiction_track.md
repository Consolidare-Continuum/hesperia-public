## What this era actually is

This is the only era in the repo where a single cross-cutting concept — "contradiction" (an
opposing-evidence signal, the negative-weight `opposes: -0.6` relation type from
`concept_relation_weights.yaml`) — is deliberately threaded through *every* layer of the stack one
task at a time: area inhibition (task1), pipeline activation (task2), topology segment dynamics
(task3), lexical/concept uncertainty (task5), negation/inhibition logic (task6), family-level
structure (task7), calibration diagnostics (task8), release gating (task9), and finally its own
spec and a centralized calibration surface (task10–11).

The reason this matters architecturally: contradiction/conflict signal is not a new layer — it is
a cross-cutting *property* that every existing layer needs to account for, because TFP clinical
discourse analysis fundamentally depends on detecting when a speaker's statements are in tension
with each other (the conflict index, CI, mentioned in `concept_relation_weights.yaml`'s header).
Rather than bolt a "contradiction module" on top of the stack as an eleventh layer, the team chose
to push the signal *down* into each existing layer's own logic — area inhibition, topology
dynamics, lexical uncertainty, family structure, calibration — one task at a time, each individually
testable and individually sealable.

## Why this ordering was the minimal right move

A cross-cutting concern threaded through every layer risks becoming an unaudited, ad hoc patch
scattered across the codebase with no single point of truth. Task9 ("add contradiction integrity
release gating and promotion verdicts") and task11 ("centralize contradiction calibration surface
and profile") are the architectural insurance against exactly that risk: even though the signal
touches many files, there is still one release gate that checks contradiction-handling integrity
before promotion, and one centralized calibration surface, rather than eleven independently-tuned
implementations. This mirrors the guard triad's own discipline (era 1): distribute the *mechanism*
where it needs to act, but centralize the *governance* of whether it acted correctly.

See [`corners/calibration.md`](../corners/calibration.md), [`corners/lexicon_resolver.md`](../corners/lexicon_resolver.md)
(`concept_relation_weights.yaml`'s `opposes` weight is the formal expression of "contradiction" at
the concept-relation level).
