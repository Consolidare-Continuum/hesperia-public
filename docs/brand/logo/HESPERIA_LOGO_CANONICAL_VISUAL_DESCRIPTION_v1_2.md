The HESPERIA logo is a geometric technical emblem. It shows a cube-like frame, an implied hexagon, and a diamond/crystal in the center.

The logo must look precise, serious, engineered, and premium.  
It must not look like a fantasy symbol, gaming logo, crypto coin, random AI network, or decorative sci-fi ornament.

## Main idea

The logo represents a stable technical system holding verified meaning inside it.

- The outside is the system.
- The inside is the meaning.
- The whole mark should feel controlled, inspectable, and intelligent.

## Outer shape

The outer shape is the most important part.

It should look like a 3D cube frame or impossible cube, but when someone looks closer, the shape should also suggest a hexagon. This is intentional.

| Reading | Means |
|---------|--------|
| **Cube** | system, structure, containment, engineering, reliability |
| **Hexagon** | order, modular logic, inspection, internal structure, HESPERIA’s hidden geometric discipline |

Do not make a plain flat hexagon.  
Do not make a normal boring cube.  
The correct form is a **cube-frame that also reads as a hexagon**.

## Center shape

In the middle, there must be a vertical diamond/crystal.

The diamond must be: centered, symmetrical, clean, faceted, slightly separated from the outer frame.

The diamond represents: verified meaning, clarity, semantic core, value, structured interpretation.

It should **not** look like: a random jewel, Ethereum/crypto symbol, fantasy crystal, magic object.

It should look like a **technical crystal held inside a system**.

## Inner geometry

The logo may include a thin inner hexagon outline or light construction lines around the diamond.

- Optional; stay subtle.
- Shows: measurement, inspection, alignment, technical structure.
- Must not overpower the main logo.

Construction circles, 60° marks, and blueprint guides are allowed only in **technical or explanatory versions**. They are not required in the primary logo.

## Six nodes

The logo should include **six** small circular nodes on the major outer points.

The nodes must be: equal in size, balanced, placed cleanly, connected visually to the outer structure.

They represent: audit points, control anchors, verification points, structural checkpoints.

Do not add too many nodes. Do not turn the logo into a neural network diagram.

## Style

The main version should be **black-and-white vector**.

Allowed: black lines, white background, grey shading, controlled hatching, subtle gradients for depth.

The logo must work **without color**.

A digital premium version may use: cyan, blue, violet, magenta, soft neon glow.

Color is secondary. The structure must still be clear in plain black and white.

## Line weight rules

| Element | Weight |
|---------|--------|
| Outer cube/hex frame | Thickest |
| Central diamond | Medium |
| Inner construction geometry | Thin |
| Blueprint guides/arcs | Very thin, optional |

Primary logo = clean emblem.  
Blueprint version = detailed technical illustration (secondary).

## Remove or avoid

Avoid: compass symbols, scale bars, decorative stars, random network meshes, bubbles, excessive glow, fake technical labels, irrelevant text, crypto look, gaming logo look, generic AI startup look, fantasy magic crystal look.

The logo must remain serious, architectural, geometric, and inspectable.

## Canonical meaning

**HESPERIA is an engineered hexagonal cube containing a semantic diamond.**

| Part | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| Outer frame | the system |
| Implied hexagon | internal order |
| Central diamond | verified meaning |
| Six nodes | audit/control anchors |
| Thin construction lines | inspection and precision |

The final logo should communicate: deterministic structure, semantic clarity, auditability, and disciplined verification.
