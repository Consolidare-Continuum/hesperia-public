HESPERIA SPEC
Deterministic Lexical-Concept Resolution and Hex Projection Layer
Version: v0.6.0-draft1
Status: Proposed
Scope: Normative
Owner: HESPERIA core
Depends on: existing Area/Family structure, existing structural aggregation, existing deterministic output discipline

====================================================================
0. TITLE
====================================================================

Deterministic Lexical-Concept Resolution and Hex Projection Layer
(DLCR-HP)

====================================================================
1. PURPOSE
====================================================================

This spec defines the missing deterministic subsystem between raw text
wording and HESPERIA structural activation.

The subsystem exists to solve one concrete problem:

raw wording must not activate families or areas directly except through
resolved concept evidence or an explicitly marked fallback path.

The subsystem introduces a first-class engine for:

- deterministic raw_text normalization
- lexical evidence extraction via Dict A
- candidate concept resolution via Dict B
- concept-to-hex projection into the 72k substrate
- local hex dynamics
- controlled upward aggregation into Family and Area levels

This layer is required because wording is noisy, concepts are not the
same as words, and family/area activation must be a projection of
resolved meaning rather than a guess from surface forms. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0} :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

====================================================================
2. NON-GOALS
====================================================================

This spec does NOT attempt to:

- solve general natural language understanding
- use opaque probabilistic models as the primary semantic mechanism
- replace the existing HESPERIA upper structure
- allow uncontrolled synonym expansion
- allow bag-of-words family classification as primary mechanism
- treat the 72k hex grid as decorative storage

The subsystem is deterministic infrastructure, not generic NLP fashion. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

====================================================================
3. ARCHITECTURAL DECISION
====================================================================

The correct HESPERIA chain SHALL be:

text/span
-> normalized lexical form
-> candidate concepts
-> resolved concept IDs
-> hex activation
-> family rollup
-> area rollup
-> interpretation / reporting

The following shortcut is FORBIDDEN as primary path:

text -> family guess

Reason:
family must be a projection of concept evidence, not the first semantic
decision point. Otherwise ambiguity leaks upward and becomes family drift. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3} :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

====================================================================
4. LAYER STACK
====================================================================

HESPERIA SHALL implement the following stack:

L0  Raw_text Normalization
L1  Lexical Evidence
L2  Concept Resolution
L3  Hex Projection
L4  Hex Dynamics
L5  Structural Aggregation
L6  Interpretation

Definitions:

L0:
raw text converted into canonical tokens, spans, and phrase candidates

L1:
Dict A markers, phrase patterns, lexical triggers, negative patterns

L2:
Dict B concept competition and deterministic winner selection

L3:
resolved concept signal mapped into fixed hex anchors / neighborhoods

L4:
spread, damping, reinforcement, concentration, dispersion, drift features

L5:
hex -> Family, Family -> Area, plus relation summaries

L6:
classification, reports, comparisons, profiles, test outputs

This layer model is normative. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5} :contentReference[oaicite:6]{index=6}

====================================================================
5. FUNDAMENTAL RULES
====================================================================

R1.
One raw_text under fixed versioned resources SHALL produce one output.

R2.
Every matched span SHALL be auditable.

R3.
Dict A SHALL propose; Dict B SHALL resolve.

R4.
Raw wording SHALL NOT directly activate families or areas except through
a clearly marked compatibility fallback mode.

R5.
Phrase-first matching SHALL take precedence over token-first guessing.

R6.
Exact match SHALL outrank lemma match; lemma match SHALL outrank stem
match; stem match SHALL outrank pattern-only match.

R7.
Hex projection SHALL occur before family/area aggregation.

R8.
Hex placement SHALL be designed, not random.

R9.
Any resource change affecting Dict A, Dict B, resolver rules, or hex
placement SHALL trigger regression tests.

R10.
All tie-breaks SHALL be stable and versioned.

These rules are required for determinism and auditability. :contentReference[oaicite:7]{index=7} :contentReference[oaicite:8]{index=8}

====================================================================
6. DATA MODEL OVERVIEW
====================================================================

The subsystem SHALL use two principal dictionaries:

Dict A = lexical evidence layer
Dict B = concept layer

Practical split:

Dict A:
surface wording, phrase triggers, variants, patterns, exclusions

Dict B:
concept IDs, definitions, hierarchy, family/area linkage, disambiguation
clues, relations, hex anchors

This separation is mandatory. Dict A does not classify. Dict B does not
guess words. :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9} :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

====================================================================
7. DICT A NORMATIVE SCHEMA
====================================================================

Each Dict A entry SHALL contain:

- entry_id
- surface_form
- normalized_form
- language
- match_type
- candidate_concept_ids
- priority
- version

Recommended required fields:

- lemma
- domain_tags
- scope_rules
- negative_patterns

Optional fields:

- hidden_labels
- transcript_variants
- misspellings
- colloquial_forms
- phrase_window_rules
- negation_sensitive
- notes

Allowed match_type values:

- exact
- phrase
- lemma
- stem
- pattern

Field definitions:

entry_id
stable unique lexical evidence identifier

surface_form
literal wording form as curated

normalized_form
canonical representation after L0 normalization

language
language code for the entry

match_type
matching rule type

candidate_concept_ids
one or more Dict B concept IDs proposed by this lexical form

priority
fixed integer used in deterministic ranking among same-level lexical hits

version
resource version tag

scope_rules
restrictions on where the entry may fire, such as phrase boundaries,
proximity conditions, domain mode, or speaker/text type

negative_patterns
blocking patterns to prevent false friends

Normative rule:
multiword entries SHALL be supported as first-class entries.

Normative rule:
Dict A entries MAY propose multiple candidate concepts.
They SHALL NOT directly decide family or area activation.

====================================================================
8. DICT B NORMATIVE SCHEMA
====================================================================

Each Dict B concept SHALL contain:

- concept_id
- preferred_label
- definition
- family_ids
- area_ids
- specificity_rank
- status
- version

Recommended required fields:

- alt_labels
- hidden_labels
- related_concept_ids
- exclusion_concept_ids
- resolver_clues
- hex_anchor
- hex_footprint_policy

Optional fields:

- broader_concept_id
- narrower_concept_ids
- associative_relation_ids
- domain_modes
- notes

Field definitions:

concept_id
stable concept identifier used across the entire engine

preferred_label
canonical human-readable name

definition
controlled concept meaning statement

family_ids
one or more linked HESPERIA family identifiers

area_ids
one or more linked HESPERIA area identifiers

specificity_rank
stable depth/specificity number used in tie-breaks

status
draft / active / deprecated / blocked

resolver_clues
explicit cues for disambiguation in context

hex_anchor
primary semantic hex location

hex_footprint_policy
rule defining local projection pattern

Normative rule:
family and area linkage SHALL follow concept identity, not precede it.

Normative rule:
cross-family membership SHALL be allowed only if explicitly declared.

Normative rule:
concept IDs SHALL be stable across versions except through controlled
deprecation/migration.

====================================================================
9. L0 INPUT NORMALIZATION
====================================================================

Purpose:
make wording boring, repeatable, and versioned. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

L0 SHALL define and freeze:

- unicode normalization
- case normalization
- punctuation policy
- tokenization policy
- sentence segmentation
- contraction handling
- abbreviation expansion
- transcript cleanup
- lemmatization policy
- stemming policy if used
- phrase candidate generation rules

L0 output SHALL include:

- canonical text
- token list
- span boundaries
- sentence boundaries
- phrase candidate spans
- normalization audit markers

Normative rules:

N1.
Normalization SHALL be deterministic and versioned.

N2.
No hidden online service or unpinned model dependency is allowed inside L0.

N3.
Speech-like inputs MAY apply stronger transcript normalization under an
explicit mode, but the mode SHALL be visible in audit output.

N4.
L0 SHALL preserve enough span mapping to trace normalized forms back to
original character offsets.

====================================================================
10. L1 LEXICAL EVIDENCE EXTRACTION
====================================================================

Purpose:
convert normalized wording into candidate lexical evidence hits.

L1 SHALL perform:

- phrase-first matching
- longest-span priority
- exact / lemma / stem / pattern matching under fixed order
- overlap handling
- negative-pattern filtering
- scope-rule validation

Match precedence SHALL be:

1. exact phrase
2. longest phrase
3. exact token
4. lemma
5. stem
6. pattern

Normative rules:

E1.
Longer valid multiword evidence SHALL outrank shorter overlapping evidence.

E2.
Overlapping hits SHALL be resolved deterministically.

E3.
Rejected lexical hits SHALL remain visible in audit logs.

E4.
L1 output SHALL contain candidate_concept_ids but SHALL NOT choose the
winning concept.

====================================================================
11. L2 CONCEPT RESOLUTION
====================================================================

Purpose:
resolve lexical evidence into stable concept IDs.

L2 is the core semantic bridge. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

For each lexical hit/span, the resolver SHALL evaluate candidate concepts
using deterministic scoring components.

Mandatory scoring components:

- lexical match strength
- span length / phrase priority
- local context overlap
- coherence with nearby already-resolved concepts
- specificity bias
- exclusion penalties
- fixed priors / stable default ordering

Resolver precedence principles:

- phrase-first
- longest-span priority
- exact over lemma over stem
- context-based disambiguation
- coherence scoring with nearby concepts
- specificity bias
- final stable tie-break ordering

These principles are mandatory. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13} :contentReference[oaicite:14]{index=14}

Example deterministic score template:

score_total =
  lexical_score
+ context_score
+ coherence_score
+ specificity_bonus
- exclusion_penalty
+ stable_prior

Exact numeric weights SHALL be versioned and externally visible.

Normative rules:

C1.
Given identical normalized raw_text and identical resources, the same winner
SHALL always be selected.

C2.
If two candidates remain tied after all explicit scores, a fixed stable
ordering SHALL break the tie.

C3.
All rejected candidates SHALL remain visible in audit output.

C4.
Resolver output SHALL include applied rules and score breakdown.

C5.
No black-box probabilistic disambiguation is allowed as primary mechanism.

====================================================================
12. L2 OUTPUT CONTRACT PER SPAN
====================================================================

For each resolved or unresolved span, output SHALL include:

- source_text_span
- source_char_start
- source_char_end
- normalized_span
- lexical_entry_ids
- candidate_concepts
- candidate_scores
- winning_concept_id
- winning_reason_codes
- rejected_reason_codes
- rule_trace
- mode
- version

If unresolved, winning_concept_id SHALL be null and unresolved_reason
SHALL be present.

This audit discipline is mandatory. :contentReference[oaicite:15]{index=15}

====================================================================
13. HEX LAYER PURPOSE
====================================================================

The 72k hex layer SHALL be treated as semantic microstate substrate,
not as cosmetic storage. :contentReference[oaicite:16]{index=16}

It exists to provide:

- microstructure
- local smoothing / denoising
- deterministic topology
- state geometry for dynamic measurement

Operational meaning:

families are provinces
concepts are named settlements
hexes are terrain cells

Without terrain, there is no movement, spread, clustering, drift, or
shape. :contentReference[oaicite:17]{index=17}

====================================================================
14. L3 CONCEPT-TO-HEX PROJECTION
====================================================================

Purpose:
project resolved concept evidence into the hex substrate before
structural aggregation.

Normative rules:

H1.
Raw words SHALL NOT map to hexes directly as semantic units.
Resolved concept IDs SHALL map to hexes.

H2.
Each active concept SHALL have one primary hex anchor.

H3.
A concept MAY project to a small fixed neighborhood footprint if declared
by policy.

H4.
Related concepts SHALL be placed near each other by design.

H5.
Families SHALL occupy broad territories.
Areas SHALL occupy macro-regions.

H6.
Cross-family semantic bridges SHALL be deliberate and sparse.

H7.
Hex placement SHALL be governed by explicit topology rules.

These are mandatory architectural rules. :contentReference[oaicite:18]{index=18}

Projection output SHALL include:

- concept_id
- hex_anchor
- activated_hexes
- activation_weights
- footprint_policy_id
- projection_trace

====================================================================
15. HEX TOPOLOGY AND DESIGN CONSTRAINTS
====================================================================

The substrate SHALL support:

- adjacency queries
- ring queries
- distance queries
- neighborhood diffusion
- boundary-aware projection
- reserved / blocked zones if governance requires

Topology design SHALL ensure:

- neighboring hexes reflect semantic adjacency
- family borders are meaningful
- relation corridors are explicit
- semantic basins and gradients can be measured

The substrate SHALL NOT become arbitrary decoration. :contentReference[oaicite:19]{index=19}

====================================================================
16. L4 HEX DYNAMICS
====================================================================

Purpose:
stabilize noisy evidence and expose structure over time or segments.

Mandatory dynamic capabilities:

- local spread
- damping
- neighborhood reinforcement
- persistence / decay
- concentration measurement
- dispersion measurement
- cluster emergence
- drift measurement
- transition measurement

This is required because speech and messy text are noisy, and hex
adjacency provides uniform locality useful for smoothing and gradient-like
operations. :contentReference[oaicite:20]{index=20} :contentReference[oaicite:21]{index=21}

L4 MAY use deterministic diffusion-style operations over the adjacency
graph, but all parameters SHALL be fixed, versioned, and auditable.

Example feature outputs:

- peak_region
- concentration_index
- dispersion_index
- cluster_count
- dominant_corridor
- drift_distance
- persistence_score
- transition_events

====================================================================
17. L5 STRUCTURAL AGGREGATION
====================================================================

Purpose:
convert hex state into Family and Area structural summaries.

Normative rule:
aggregation SHALL consume hex state, not raw wording and not unresolved
lexical hits.

L5 SHALL compute at minimum:

- concept activation summary
- family activation vector
- area activation vector
- cross-family relation signals
- uncertainty / degradation markers

Normative rules:

S1.
Family activation SHALL be derived from concept/hex evidence.

S2.
Area activation SHALL be derived from family/hex evidence.

S3.
Compatibility fallback mode, if present, SHALL be clearly marked in
output and SHALL NOT be the default.

====================================================================
18. L6 INTERPRETATION
====================================================================

Purpose:
produce product/report level outputs from structural state.

Examples:

- matched spans
- rejected spans
- concept activations
- concept competition logs
- hex state summaries
- dominant family regions
- area profile
- transition path across segments
- stability metrics
- comparison between texts or sessions

These outputs are the target instrumentation model. :contentReference[oaicite:22]{index=22} :contentReference[oaicite:23]{index=23}

====================================================================
19. SCIENTIFIC MODE VS SPEECH/CLINICAL MODE
====================================================================

HESPERIA SHALL support at least two policy modes under one architecture:

A. technical/scientific mode
- narrower terminology
- higher precision lexical-concept mapping
- less smoothing
- stronger exact phrase priority

B. speech/clinical mode
- stronger normalization
- more phrase handling
- more context competition
- more smoothing
- more trajectory emphasis

Reason:
science text behaves better because terminology is more stable.
Random speech is more ambiguous, incomplete, idiomatic, and drifting. :contentReference[oaicite:24]{index=24}

Normative rule:
mode differences SHALL change policies/resources, not core architecture.

====================================================================
20. ASSOCIATION AND SALIENCE METHODS
====================================================================

The subsystem MAY use deterministic association/salience methods to
support interpretation and resolver quality, provided they are fixed and
auditable.

Allowed examples:

- TF-IDF-style salience weighting
- PMI / co-occurrence association
- fixed-frequency priors
- hierarchy-aware coherence boosts

These methods SHALL support, not replace, deterministic concept
resolution. :contentReference[oaicite:25]{index=25}

====================================================================
21. FAILURE MODES
====================================================================

The system SHALL explicitly detect and expose the following failure modes:

- unresolved span
- overlapping lexical conflict
- conflicting concept tie beyond allowed threshold
- missing concept anchor
- missing family linkage
- missing area linkage
- blocked by negative pattern
- degraded fallback activation
- incompatible mode/resource selection
- regression drift detected

Failure markers SHALL appear in audit output.

====================================================================
22. OUTPUT CONTRACTS
====================================================================

Two output classes SHALL exist:

A. internal-rich audit output
B. public-safe summarized output

A. internal-rich audit output SHALL include:
- normalization trace
- lexical hits
- rejected lexical hits
- candidate concepts
- score breakdowns
- winner decisions
- hex projection trace
- hex dynamics summary
- family and area vectors
- degradation and failure flags

B. public-safe output SHALL include:
- deterministic summaries
- selected structural indicators
- optional compact matched concept lists
- version tags
- no sensitive internal scoring unless enabled

Normative rule:
the public-safe output SHALL be derivable from internal output, not
generated by a separate semantic path.

====================================================================
23. VERSIONING
====================================================================

The following SHALL be versioned independently and jointly referenceable:

- Dict A version
- Dict B version
- normalization version
- resolver rule version
- hex topology version
- aggregation version
- output schema version

Each run SHALL emit:

- engine_version
- resource_versions
- mode
- deterministic_signature

====================================================================
24. REGRESSION AND CALIBRATION
====================================================================

The subsystem SHALL be tested like a compiler, not like vague analytics. :contentReference[oaicite:26]{index=26} :contentReference[oaicite:27]{index=27}

Mandatory test artifacts:

- gold concept mapping corpus
- ambiguity corpus
- phrase-priority corpus
- overlap conflict corpus
- speech-noise corpus
- hex projection corpus
- family rollup corpus

Minimum release gate requirements:

G1.
Known gold cases SHALL retain expected concept winners unless explicitly
version-changed.

G2.
Phrase-first precedence SHALL be verified.

G3.
Stable tie-break ordering SHALL be verified.

G4.
No concept may be emitted without valid family linkage unless marked
degraded.

G5.
No resolved concept may be emitted without valid hex anchor unless marked
degraded.

G6.
Regression diffs SHALL be reviewable.

====================================================================
25. IMPLEMENTATION PHASES
====================================================================

Phase 1 - Freeze schema discipline
Deliver:
- lexical entry schema
- concept schema
- family linkage schema
- area linkage schema
- resolver output schema

Phase 2 - Build small deterministic resolver
Start with one bounded domain, recommended:
TFP / clinical concept pack
Initial target:
- 100-300 concepts
- 5-15 lexical forms each
- explicit exclusions
- deterministic score formula

Phase 3 - Map into bounded hex region
Do not scale to full 72k immediately.
Prove:
- nearby concepts behave sensibly
- smoothing improves stability
- aggregation remains interpretable

Phase 4 - Add segment trajectory features
Segment by sentence, turn, or paragraph and measure movement across hex zones.

Phase 5 - Scale to full substrate
Scale only after local proofs and regression discipline hold.

This implementation order is normative guidance. :contentReference[oaicite:28]{index=28} :contentReference[oaicite:29]{index=29}

28. ACCEPTANCE CRITERIA FOR v0.6.0 FIRST SHIP
====================================================================

The subsystem SHALL be considered minimally shippable when all are true:

- raw wording no longer directly activates families by default
- Dict A and Dict B schemas are frozen and validated
- normalization is deterministic and versioned
- phrase-first lexical matching works
- resolver produces auditable per-span output
- first concept pack exists and passes gold tests
- resolved concepts map to bounded hex anchors
- hex state aggregates upward into family/area vectors
- regression gate blocks silent drift
- public-safe output remains deterministic

====================================================================
29. HARD PROHIBITIONS
====================================================================

The following are prohibited as default engine behavior:

- direct word-to-family activation
- uncontrolled fuzzy synonym expansion
- hidden online resolution services
- unversioned lexical resources
- untracked manual concept winner overrides
- random or ad hoc hex placement
- non-auditable primary disambiguation
- silent regression drift

====================================================================
30. BOTTOM LINE
====================================================================

HESPERIA SHALL treat wording-to-concept resolution as a first-class
engine.

Once this rule is enforced:

- Dict A becomes meaningful
- Dict B becomes actionable
- the 72k hex layer gains real purpose
- family and area aggregation stop being guesswork
- speech classification becomes difficult but structurally tractable

Single most important rule:

Do not allow raw wording to activate families directly unless routed
through resolved concept evidence or an explicitly marked fallback path. :contentReference[oaicite:30]{index=30}
