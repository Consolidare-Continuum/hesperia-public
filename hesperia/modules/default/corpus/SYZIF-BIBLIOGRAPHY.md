# Syzif corpus — bibliography (titles only)

The `default` module (`hesperia.modules.default`) is calibrated against the **syzif** reference library.
The library's full text is **not** distributed in this public repository. The texts are third-party copyrighted works,
held privately by CONSOLIDARE CONTINUUM S.R.L. for internal calibration and testing only.
Only the identity of each work is published here, for transparency and reproducibility of provenance.
